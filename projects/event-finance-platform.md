# Event Finance Platform

A SaaS platform that replaces spreadsheet-based budgeting, supplier management and sponsor reporting for event organisers.

**Client:** A South African events promoter running major music festivals · **Role:** Solution architect · **Year:** 2025 · **Status:** Specified & designed

## The problem

Festival organisers managed budgets, suppliers, payments and multi-partner profit splits in shared spreadsheets. Year-on-year comparisons were slow, sponsors had no view of their ROI, and sensitive supplier data was hard to protect.

## What I did

- Led requirements with organisers, partners and sponsors, and turned them into a full platform specification.
- Designed role-based access so each partner, supplier and sponsor sees only what they should.
- Planned ticketing integration for live revenue, supplier quote-to-invoice workflows and reusable event templates.
- Produced the architecture, data model, security design and delivery plan.

## Outcome

- Targets set with the client: 75% less manual reporting and 90% faster budget comparisons.

**Built with (planned):** React, TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Redis, AWS
