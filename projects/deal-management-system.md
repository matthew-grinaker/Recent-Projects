# Deal Management System

A multi-tenant platform that runs property transactions end to end, from first client contact to registration.

**Client:** South African real estate agencies · **Role:** Architect & lead developer · **Year:** 2025 · **Status:** Live

## The problem

A property deal involves buyers, sellers, agents, attorneys and conveyancers, plus deposits, commission splits and compliance. Agencies tracked this across spreadsheets and email, with no live view of branch or agent performance.

## What I did

- Designed a multi-tenant, multi-branch platform with role-based access for agents, conveyancers, finance and management.
- Built the full deal lifecycle, CRM, conveyancing stages and automatic multi-agent commission calculations.
- Added 30+ built-in reports and live business dashboards.
- Encrypted all personal data and kept full audit trails.

## Outcome

- 200+ API endpoints and 50+ entities in production, with AES-256 encryption on all personal data.
- One system replaced spreadsheet tracking for deals, commissions and reporting.

**Built with:** ASP.NET Core, Entity Framework Core, SQL Server, Angular, Ionic, Azure DevOps
