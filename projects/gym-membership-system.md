# Legends Gym App

A membership and access-control system for Legends Gym: a web back office plus tablet check-in at the door.

**Client:** Legends Gym · **Role:** Solution designer & developer · **Year:** 2025–2026 · **Status:** Live

## The problem

The gym ran membership on paper and SMS. Contracts expired unnoticed, check-in wasn't controlled, and the owners had no view of attendance or revenue.

## What I did

- Built a web back office for members, digital contracts, renewals and billing.
- Built tablet check-in that approves or denies entry instantly based on membership status, with expiry warnings and a full access log.
- Added reporting on revenue, attendance and peak hours, and replaced SMS with email.

## Outcome

- Live at Legends Gym: digital contracts, automatic renewal reminders and controlled entry replace paper and SMS.
- Designed to cut manual admin by up to 90%.

**Built with:** Web app with real-time WebSocket check-in, tablet kiosk, email notifications
