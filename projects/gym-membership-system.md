# Gym Membership System

A membership and access-control system for a gym: a web back office plus tablet check-in at the door.

**Client:** An independent gym · **Role:** Solution designer · **Year:** 2025 · **Status:** Proposal

## The problem

The gym ran membership on paper and SMS: contracts expired unnoticed, check-in wasn't controlled, and owners had no view of attendance or revenue.

## What I did

- Designed a web back office for members, digital contracts, renewals and billing.
- Designed tablet check-in with instant approve/deny based on membership status, expiry warnings and access logs.
- Planned reporting on revenue, attendance and peak hours, and replaced SMS with email.
- Produced the proposal and screen designs for the client.

## Outcome

- Targeted a 90% reduction in manual admin and real-time control over who enters.

**Built with (planned):** Web app with real-time WebSocket check-in and tablet kiosk
